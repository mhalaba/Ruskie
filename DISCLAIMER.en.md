# Disclaimer

Open-source gazetteer of publicly known Russian Armed Forces **home garrisons**, **defense-plant towns** (city centroid), **UAV centres/plants** (city centroid) and **named EW systems**.

This is **not** a battlefield tracker, targeting product, or Ukrainian-theater map. No coordinates finer than a settlement centroid / public HQ address. No ammo/fuel dumps, silos, UAV or missile launch pads, barracks interiors, plant gates, jammer sites, frequencies, satellite-measured object GPS, or PII below district/army commander.

Units operating away from home keep the RF garrison (Crimea: `occupied_ukraine`, layer off by default). The 3rd and 51st combined-arms armies (Donetsk/Luhansk HQ in open tables) have **no pin**.

Authors are not responsible for misuse. See `last_verified` and the map footer date.
